Introduction

I design how digital products work and the thinking behind them — the research, systems thinking, information architecture, and user flows that hold them together — with a particular interest in where AI fits naturally into that process. I like finding the moments in a product where AI can genuinely help, without losing sight of the person using it. Currently completing an MA in Design, Technology & AI in Berlin, building on a design and media background from the UK and Germany.

About

Carlotta Freytag

I'm a UX and product designer. Across my projects, I've developed strengths that cover the full arc of a product — research, structure, interaction, and interface. I design with, through, and for AI: my work starts from what a person actually needs, then looks for the specific places in a system where AI can meaningfully help. I care about the craft of good design first; AI is one of the more interesting tools I get to design with, not the whole story. I'm currently completing an MA in Design, Technology & AI at Media Design Hochschule Berlin, building on a media production background from the UK and Germany.
